AI Meal Planner

AI-powered personalized recipes, wherever your shoppers are.

On-demand meal planning on your site, built around what each shopper needs.

Personalized meal inspiration

Recipes tailored to each shopper’s dietary needs, lifestyle, budget, and health preferences.

Multilingual

English and Spanish today, more languages on the way.

Conversational planning

Shoppers ask for meal ideas, then refine them in conversation, like swapping an ingredient or reducing servings.

Learns from interactions

Remembers what shoppers share, so recommendations get more relevant over time.

Connects meals to shopping

Lists the ingredients and helps shoppers build a basket on your website, turning inspiration into sales.

Relevant savings

Coupons and savings appear alongside meal and product recommendations, helping shoppers save while encouraging additional purchases.

Your brand, your look

Add your own branding if you’d like, so it feels like part of your site.

Why it matters to you

Shoppers already expect recipe ideas and personalization when they shop online, and grocers who deliver it earn more repeat visits. AI Meal Planner gives shoppers a reason to come back to your site, without adding work for your team.

AI Picks to Inbox

Smart recommendations, right in their inbox.

Personalized meal plans, replenishment reminders, and offers delivered straight to shoppers’ inboxes, automatically.

Personalized communications

Uses purchase history, shopping patterns, preferences and interactions with the Meal Planner to make each email more relevant to the individual shopper.

Anticipatory recommendations

Uses shopping behavior to identify products, meals and offers a shopper may be ready for, helping grocers stay relevant between trips instead of waiting for the next visit.

Meal inspiration

Delivers meal ideas based on individual shopper interests and behavior, giving shoppers new reasons to engage with their grocer.

Replenishment reminders

Helps shoppers remember products they may be ready to purchase again based on previous shopping patterns, creating another opportunity to drive a return trip.

Relevant products and savings

Promotes products, offers and savings based on what is available at the shopper’s store, making recommendations useful while supporting additional purchases.

Keeps the retailer at the center

Delivers personalized engagement within the retailer’s own branded experience, helping independent grocers stay top of mind while maintaining the customer relationship and first-party data.

Why it matters to you

Most of your business still happens in the store, and AI Picks to Inbox is how you keep loyal shoppers connected to it. It reaches them where they already are, their inbox, with something personal enough to bring them back, whether that means another trip to your store or a visit to your site.

AI Chat to Shop

Give shoppers real answers, without needing to replace your website or app.

A conversational AI assistant that helps shoppers search, build a basket, and shop around their own preferences and allergies.

AI-powered chat

Shoppers can ask questions in natural language and receive helpful answers without having to navigate the site on their own.

Personalized search

Helps shoppers find relevant information and products based on what they are looking for without searching through multiple pages, reducing friction in the digital shopping experience to make it easier and more intuitive.

Guides shoppers to the right experience

Acts as a conversational entry point to other capabilities powered by EVERYAISLE, be it shopping assistance or personalized meal planning.

Keeps shoppers within the grocer’s experience

Provides AI assistance directly within the retailer’s branded website, helping the grocer remain the destination for the shopping journey.

Flexible deployment

Chat to Shop can be added to a grocer’s existing website or deployed as part of a Smart Store digital storefront, giving retailers flexibility to enhance their current experience or build on the EVERYAISLE platform.

Why it matters to you

Shoppers want help finding exactly what they need, fast, and AI Chat to Shop delivers that without asking you to change anything else. If you’re not ready to replace your storefront, or you’re locked into a contract with another provider, it delivers real personalization now, working with your existing inventory and ordering systems.

AI Smart Store

A digital storefront that updates itself, so you don’t have to.

A dynamic, self-managed digital storefront built for grocers who don’t have the time or team to keep their site current.

Personalized storefronts

Each shopper receives an experience shaped around their preferences and shopping behavior, creating a digital storefront that is more relevant to them. No two shoppers see the same storefront.

Relevant product recommendations

Helps shoppers discover products suited to their needs and interests while giving grocers more opportunities to surface relevant products.

Personalized savings

Surfaces coupons and offers relevant to each shopper, helping customers find savings while putting the grocer’s promotions in front of shoppers who are more likely to value them.

Product discovery

Highlights trending products and new items available from the store, giving shoppers more reasons to explore and giving grocers additional opportunities to promote products.

Current and self-managed

Smart Store integrates with transaction logs and circulars to automatically update inventory, promotions and recommendations, reducing the manual maintenance required to keep the digital storefront current.

Language support

Up-to-date catalogues, typo-tolerant searches and more are currently available to use via Smart Store in English and Spanish, with a broader range of language support on the horizon.

Why it matters to you

Most independent storefronts look the way they do because there’s no time to manage them, so the job gets outsourced or ignored. AI Smart Store removes that burden entirely: built to run itself, while giving every shopper a personalized experience the way national chains already do.
